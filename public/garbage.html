Chute trunking

Cut from flat sheet and machine-rolled into an accurate cylinder. Vertical seams are lock-seamed or welded, depending on material and gauge, for smooth watertight joints. The inner surface has no projections to slow falling refuse.

Components, from roof to refuse room

Every Awaan chute is built from the same set of parts. They are listed in the order they sit in the building, top to bottom.

At the roof and top floor

Ventilation, cleaning and maintenance

Vent and exhaust fan

A roof-level fan changes the air about 50 times an hour and stops odours or aerosol gases escaping through the hoppers. Fitted at the top of the chute, for vent pipes of 230 mm (9″) or more.

Linen chute doors

Side-hung doors with large openings for bagged linen, fully self-closing on a hydraulic closer. We recommend time-delay electric interlocks for operator safety.

  • 450×450 mm opening
  • Stainless steel hinges
  • Hydraulic self-closer
  • Time-delay interlocks
  • Separate or master-keyed locks
  • “Linen only” labels on request
